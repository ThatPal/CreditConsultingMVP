## Client walkthrough

1. Start at `/app`; confirm one dominant current focus and factual goal/profile/Plan context.
2. Follow Credit Center → Review → Profile → Analysis → Plan. Verify meaning, source/as-of, owner and next step.
3. Open Cards → Explore → a detail → Save for research → Wishlist. Confirm research is not described as approval.
4. Open Services and Purchase History; inspect price/payment/entitlement language without raw provider enums.
5. Open Application Rounds → Fall 2026 → Strategy → Schedule → Live. Confirm these screens agree and Live says connected only while the realtime transport is connected.
6. Continue Results → Follow-Up → Analysis. In the current pre-end fixture, confirm waiting/prerequisite language rather than invented outcomes.
7. Review Major Readiness, Notifications, Documents, Support, Account and Security. Test a safe notification deep link and browser Back.

## Consultant walkthrough

## Admin walkthrough

1. At `/admin`, verify only degraded modules appear in Current exceptions—normal active inventory must not.
2. Inspect Users, Access Grants, Sessions, Audit and Security. Open one governed preview and cancel it; verify scope, timing, reversibility and audit consequence.
3. Inspect Products, Payments and PayPal/Stripe/BofA. Confirm no credentials or unsupported BofA capability appears.
4. Inspect Card Catalog/Insights, AI Jobs/Processes, Sources, Workflow Rules, Notifications and Integrations.
5. Inspect Scheduled Jobs, Retention, Reports, Settings and System Health. Confirm a current degraded snapshot says **Confirmed**, not **Stale**, and links to owning remediation.

## Narrow, keyboard and recovery checks

- Repeat representative Client `/app/plan`, CRM Work Queue/Client 360, and Admin Payments/System Health at a phone-width viewport. Navigation must remain reachable; no important table may force document-width overflow.
- Tab through header, context rail, collection and primary action. Use Arrow Up/Down in a bounded collection; open/cancel a dialog and confirm focus returns.
- With reduced motion enabled, content remains understandable without animation.
- Refresh a deep route; temporarily interrupt API access and restore it. The shell must preserve location and show loading/retry language—never a blank document.

## Acceptance prompts

- Is the screen composed around its actual job rather than a stack of equivalent cards?
- Is the most important factual state visually dominant?
- Does every visualization have real source data and an accessible text equivalent?
- Can the user explain where they are, what the state means, who owns it, the next action or waiting condition, and recovery?
- Are normal work and exceptions visually distinct? Are destructive/governed effects previewed?
- Can a user complete the journey without losing client, Round, filter or return context?

Record any physical-review variance by route, role, viewport, exact action and observed state. Do not accept a screenshot or static render as proof of a live command.
