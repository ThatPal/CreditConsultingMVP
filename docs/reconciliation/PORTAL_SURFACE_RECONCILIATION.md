## Flow, overlay and global-state obligations

WF-01–18, MO-01–20 and GS-01–28 are not 66 new database entities or necessarily routes. Their source command, commit boundary, retry behavior, focus/return and mobile state must be implemented inside the owning surface. In particular Review update, current-card edit, Action response, request-help, purchase confirmation and rescheduling reuse canonical records. No frontend flow may grant access from a return URL, verify its own Action, replace an immutable submission, or turn notification acknowledgement into task completion.

Desktop/mobile status for every current route remains explicit in the generated JSON. Only the U1 reference captures have fresh U0 browser evidence; other screens require visual/state verification in their owning wave. This distinction does not prevent an inventory disposition, but prevents claiming product completion from the mapping.
